I am **Qiao Xiao**, a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by [Prof. Yangqiu Song](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~yqsong/).

My research interests include reinforcement learning, GraphRAG, and tool discovery for large language model agents. My recent work includes [AutoGraph-R1](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1070/), [SING](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16591), and [TERAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18667).

Before joining HKUST, I completed a master's degree in Computer Science at Cornell University and a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering at The Ohio State University.

## Publications
